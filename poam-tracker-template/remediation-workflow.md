# Remediation Workflow

## Objective
This document outlines a simple workflow for managing identified weaknesses from discovery through remediation, validation, and closure.

## Workflow Steps

### 1. Identify the weakness
Document the issue clearly, including what was found, what control or process is affected, and when the issue was identified.

### 2. Assess and prioritize
Determine the severity or risk level of the weakness so remediation work can be prioritized appropriately.

### 3. Assign an owner
Assign responsibility for corrective action to an individual or team and establish an expected target date.

### 4. Define remediation actions
Document the corrective steps required to address the weakness and reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

### 5. Track progress
Update the POA&M entry with milestone progress, status updates, blockers, and revised dates when needed.

### 6. Validate remediation
Review the completed action and supporting evidence to confirm the weakness has been addressed effectively.

### 7. Close the item
Mark the item closed only after validation is complete and the supporting documentation is retained.

## Notes
This sample workflow is intended to demonstrate a practical, structured approach to corrective action tracking in an RMF-aligned environment.
