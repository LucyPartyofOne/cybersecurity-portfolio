# Reporting and Escalation

## Purpose
This document provides a simple example of how monitoring results, open findings, and significant issues may be reported and escalated in an RMF-aligned environment.

## Reporting Approach
Recurring monitoring results should be summarized in a way that helps stakeholders understand current control status, open issues, recent changes, and remediation progress.

## Typical Reporting Elements
Reports may include:
- Open findings and status trends
- Significant changes affecting control posture
- Overdue remediation items
- Documentation updates completed or pending
- Issues requiring leadership awareness or action

## Escalation Triggers
Issues may require escalation when they involve:
- High-risk or unresolved weaknesses
- Missed remediation deadlines
- Significant control failures
- Repeated findings with no sustained improvement
- Changes that materially affect system risk

## Escalation Approach
When escalation is needed, the issue should be routed to the appropriate owner or leadership level with a clear summary of the problem, current status, expected action, and any deadlines for response.

## Notes
This sample document is intended to demonstrate portfolio-level understanding of reporting and escalation concepts within a monitoring program.
